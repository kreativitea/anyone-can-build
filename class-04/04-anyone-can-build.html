Go live
Prod, first: the page, empty, really on the internet
5 min
  • First, decide who builds: that is the engineer.
  • 💻 Engineer: a new repository from the starter template, anyone-can-build-split-bill-demo
  • Turn on publishing (Settings → Pages), the same as last week
  • Lane A: clone it, and start Claude Code in that folder  ·  Lane B: stay in the browser
  • Read your address out loud. Your partner opens it on their phone or laptop.
The first publish takes a minute or two. After that, every push is live.

Talk
Idea and Design: what problem, for whom, and what it will be
4 min
  • Everybody's problem: A group eats out, and the restaurant brings one bill. How much does each person pay?
  • 📱 Product person: draw the screen you want, and hide it. Then: the last time you split a bill, what went wrong? And your success line.
  • 💻 Engineer: ask, listen, and write their words and their success line in your tracker. One screen · no login · no saved data.
  • ☐ their problem, not their information   ☐ no names, no student numbers, no contact details
  • ☐ nothing private into the AI   ☐ public means public — your page is already live
Their words, not better words.
Anyone Can Build · Class 417 / 25
Play
Build · Prod · Analysis — and around again
40 min
  • 💻 Engineer: one change per turn — prompt · read · commit · push
  • ❯ [one thing they asked for, in their words]. Only that. Explain each part, because I have to be able to read it.
  • 📱 Product person: refresh · try it before anyone says a word · then say what you see, and what you want next
  • Edit · Run · Read · Debug · Commit — learning mode stays on
  • 📱 While it builds: keep trying what is live, and choose the one thing to ask for next.
No peeking, no pointing. Only words cross the table.
Anyone Can Build · Class 418 / 25
Last call
Analysis: did it work?
4 min
  • No more changes. One last refresh, one last silent try.
  • 📱 Product person, out loud: success line met — yes or no?
  • Then put your drawing next to the page: what came back different from what you meant?
  • 💻 Engineer: write both answers in your tracker, and the one thing they would still change
Whatever is live now is the answer.
