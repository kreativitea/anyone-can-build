Demos

  • Easy Peasy Vacation Planning

    Five places around Kansai, sorted by how far they are from wherever you are standing, and an hour-by-hour day built from them — including the travel between the stops, not just there and back. It tells you whether the day fits, and when it does not, how many of them would. Add more places from the list. It fetches nothing: everything it knows is written into the one file, and the only network use is the map links you choose to open.

  • Earthquakes around Japan↗

    Every recorded earthquake of magnitude 4 and above around Japan since 2016 — fourteen and a half thousand of them, sized by magnitude and coloured by depth. Move the sliders, play the years, or cut a cross-section down through the subduction zone. Built in front of the room in Class 1.
